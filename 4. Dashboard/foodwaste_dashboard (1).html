      Reading for leadership

      ESG framing
      Intervention point

      The household is the ESG lever, not the supply chain

      Household waste (569M t) is nearly 2.4× retail and food service combined, and is the largest single source in 212 of 214 territories. Upstream loss‑reduction programmes will under‑deliver unless paired with consumer behaviour and post‑retail‑date campaigns.

      Concentration risk

      Three countries carry 38% of global tonnage

      China, India and Nigeria alone account for roughly 354M of the 931M tonnes. A global target is only as credible as the monitoring plan in these three markets — worth flagging in any group‑level Scope 3 / value‑chain waste narrative.

      Data governance

      Don't cite country rankings without a caveat

      91% of estimates sit in the Low or Very Low confidence bands. This is a modelled dataset, useful for materiality screening and target‑setting direction, but it should not substitute for site‑level measurement in assurance‑grade ESG disclosures.
